Connect to Google Sheets

Simple setup - no API key needed!

1. Make your Google Sheet public (Share → Anyone with link)
2. Paste your sheet URL or just the ID:
docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/[ID]/edit

The GID is in the URL after #gid= (usually 0 for first sheet)
